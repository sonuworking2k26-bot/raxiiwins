## Simple rules

- 18+ only.
- Money deposited can be lost.
- Tips and “AI predictors” do not remove chance.
- Never borrow to play.

## Set limits before you play

| Limit | Example habit |
|-------|----------------|
| Daily loss | Hard rupee cap; stop when hit |
| Time | Alarm after 30–40 minutes |
| Deposit | No “one more recharge” after the cap |
| Alcohol | Do not play when judgement is down |

## When to stop

- Chasing a loss with bigger stakes
- Hiding spend from family
- Borrowing or using someone else’s UPI
- Feeling sick when the app is closed

Step away. Delete shortcuts if needed. Read the [scam checklist](/guides/scam-checklist) if pressure is coming from a chat agent.

## Help mindset

This site publishes guides only. For serious gambling harm, talk to someone you trust and look up local support resources in India. Returning to [What is Raxi Win](/) for product curiosity is fine — depositing under stress is not.

## Bottom line

Treat Raxi Win style lobbies as entertainment with a fixed budget. Limits first, play second.
