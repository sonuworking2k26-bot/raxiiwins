## WinGo colour rounds

Typical pattern across colour lobbies:

1. Pick a room length (for example 1 min / 3 min / 5 min).
2. Choose colour, number, and/or big-small style options the UI shows.
3. Confirm before the lock timer.
4. Wait for the result strip to update.

No tip channel can guarantee the next colour. Treat “prediction bots” as marketing noise.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which game should beginners open?

Many people try a low-stake WinGo room first to learn timers — still chance-based.

### Do gift codes change odds?

No. Codes may add balance or coupons; they do not make rounds fairer or unfairer.

### Where are screenshots from?

Representative lobby UI for teaching. Live hosts can rearrange tiles.
