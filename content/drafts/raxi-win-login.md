## What you need to sign in

- The **exact** mobile number from signup (SIM preferably in this phone if OTP appears).
- Your password, typed carefully — watch auto-correct.
- A stable connection (avoid flipping Wi-Fi mid-submit).
- The same host / app build you registered on when possible.

---

## How to complete Raxi Win Login

1. Open the official login destination from a bookmark or the Login button on this site.
2. Check the address bar before typing.
3. Stay on the **Phone Number** tab unless you registered by email.
4. Confirm **+91**, then enter your 10-digit number.
5. Enter your password (use the eye icon to verify).
6. Optional: tick **Remember password** only on a phone only you use.
7. Tap **Log in**. Complete OTP if asked.
8. Confirm the account / UID on the home or account screen is yours.

If you do not have an account yet, use [Register](/raxi-win-register) instead of guessing passwords.

## Phone vs email tab

The login screen often shows **Phone Number** and **Email** tabs. Use the method you actually registered with. Mixing them is a common reason “password incorrect” appears even when you are sure of the password.

---

## Forgot password

Use **Forgot password?** on the same login page. Typical flow: enter registered mobile → SMS OTP → set a new password.

- Enter OTP only on that recovery screen.
- Never send the code to someone who DMs “support.”
- If you no longer control the SIM, use the host’s published support channel — not a random Telegram bot.

## How to keep your account safe

- Unique password — not your bank PIN pattern.
- No OTP sharing, ever.
- Avoid “login fix” APKs and remote-control apps.
- Log out on shared devices.
- Verify URLs after every forwarded link ([scam checklist](/guides/scam-checklist)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need for Raxi Win Login?

Usually your registered mobile number and password. Some sessions also ask for OTP.

### Can I login on a new phone?

Yes if you still control the number and password. You may need OTP on the new device.

### Why is login not working?

Most often: typo, wrong tab (email vs phone), expired OTP, or a different host than the one you registered on.

### Is it safe to share my OTP?

No. OTPs prove control of your SIM. Support that is real will not need you to paste codes into chat.
